Your heroes do the fighting. Your job is getting the gold home.

Think Idle Miner Tycoon, but your workers are wizards, archers, beastcallers and a celestial phoenix. Send them into an ever-deepening stack of dungeons, watch the gold pile up, and keep your lift and cart hauling it to the treasury. Collect heroes, power them up, and pick the right crew for every dungeon. When the gold slows, ascend for a permanent boost and dig deeper.

An idle hero collector for iOS, Android and Steam. Free to play, and playable now on TestFlight.

Heroes and monsters

More than thirty heroes to collect, across six rarities. Fighters earn more gold, haulers move it faster, merchants cut upgrade costs, timekeepers skip time, and heralds recharge the other heroes’ ultimates. Every dungeon has its own family of monsters.

The core loop

Fight

Heroes defeat each dungeon’s monsters, and every win drops gold.

→

Haul

A lift and a cart carry the gold up to the treasury.

→

Upgrade

Gold levels up the dungeons, the lift and cart, and the heroes.

→

Go deeper

Opening the next dungeon brings a big jump in gold.

Then it starts again, and the guild keeps earning while you’re away.

The long game

Collect. Chests drop hero cards, which unlock ultimates and auto-fire and, over weeks, mastery.

Conquer. Paying to conquer a dungeon multiplies its gold for good.

Ascend. Starting the run over gives a permanent gold boost, and heroes keep their levels.
